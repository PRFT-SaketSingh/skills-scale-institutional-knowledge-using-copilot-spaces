# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Enablement Leads help keep work flowing across teams by managing dependency health, project board hygiene, and milestone readiness. They act as operational force multipliers to reduce friction in execution.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain backlog health and work-in-progress visibility
- Track dependencies, risks, and cross-team handoffs
- Coordinate milestone readiness and delivery checkpoints
- Help unblock teams by surfacing blockers early and escalating appropriately
- Support planning sessions and recovery meetings when delivery slips

### Goals
- Improve predictability across delivery work
- Reduce bottlenecks and avoid hidden dependencies
- Keep teams aligned on priorities and execution pace

### Typical Communication
- Dependency reviews and milestone check-ins
- Delivery syncs with PMs and engineering leads
- Project board updates and blocker summaries

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Project Managers to maintain schedule and operational visibility
- Collaborates with Developers and Product Managers to assess scope, sequencing, and delivery risks
- Escalates unresolved cross-team dependencies to sponsors or leadership when needed

---

## Stakeholder Liaisons

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons are the bridge between the delivery team and external or cross-functional stakeholders. They ensure communication remains clear, timely, and aligned with the project’s goals.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain stakeholder lists and communication plans
- Share status updates, decisions, and key milestones with sponsors and partners
- Gather feedback from stakeholders and route it to the appropriate owner
- Help coordinate approvals and change communications
- Surface stakeholder concerns early so they can be addressed before impact grows

### Goals
- Reduce surprises and improve trust with stakeholders
- Ensure the right audience gets the right information at the right time
- Keep business context visible to the delivery team

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and project updates
- Release readiness and decision communications
- Cross-functional status summaries and escalation notes

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Project Managers to keep communication plans consistent and timely
- Supports Product Managers by translating customer or business feedback into actionable inputs
- Coordinates with Developers and Quality Advocates when stakeholder expectations affect scope, release timing, or acceptance criteria

---

## Quality Advocates

### Role Summary
Quality Advocates protect the definition of done and the quality bar for releases. They help ensure that customer value is delivered without sacrificing reliability, usability, or operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Review acceptance criteria and quality gates for completeness
- Coordinate testing plans, smoke checks, and release readiness reviews
- Support triage of defects, regressions, and root-cause analysis
- Verify that non-functional requirements are considered before launch
- Partner with engineering and product to maintain quality expectations over time

### Goals
- Improve release confidence and reduce avoidable defects
- Ensure quality is built into delivery, not added at the end
- Support sustainable, reliable product operations

### Typical Communication
- QA reviews and release readiness checklists
- Defect triage and risk discussions
- Post-release review and retrospective follow-up

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to validate design, testability, and quality expectations
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and customer impact
- Supports Project Managers in assessing whether release criteria are ready for deployment

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- New personas complement the core roles by clarifying accountability, improving communication, and making dependency and quality ownership more explicit.
- Delivery Enablement Leads, Stakeholder Liaisons, and Quality Advocates help teams scale from small projects to more complex cross-functional work without losing clarity or accountability.
